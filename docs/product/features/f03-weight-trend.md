# F03. Weight with trend

- Release: v0.1
- Status: Approved 2026-10-07

## What

Log one weight per day and see the trend.

## Rules

- One weight per day; editing replaces it
- The app reminds the measuring rule: after waking, after the bathroom, before eating
- The chart shows a moving trend next to the raw value

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An outlier day (+1.5 kg) moves the trend only slightly
