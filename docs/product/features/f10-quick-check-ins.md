# F10. Quick check-ins

- Release: v0.1
- Status: Approved 2026-10-07

## What

Body and mood in one or two taps.

## Rules

- Bathroom: Bristol 1–7 drawings + "gas only"
- Bloating relative to your normal: better / usual / worse
- **Highest stress felt today** (the peak), energy and mood as colour faces
- Hydration: water and zero-calorie drinks are tapped here as approximate glasses (≈ 250 ml). Drinks with calories logged in meals appear here automatically with their meal marked. Tapping a caloric drink here (soft drink, juice, energy drink) creates its food entry in the current meal, so calories are never lost or doubled. Alcohol is logged but not counted. Urine colour (clear / yellow / dark)
- Daily drinks goal = 80% of the EFSA total water intake (men 2.5 L, women 2.0 L) in glasses, rounded up (men 8, women 7) + climate extra from the profile (warm +2, temperate and cold 0) + 2 glasses with "I sweated a lot today". Water from food is not tracked
- Bathroom also offers "didn't go today"
- Every check-in stores its time

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Any check-in takes at most two taps
