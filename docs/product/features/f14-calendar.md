# F14. Calendar

- Release: v0.1
- Status: Approved 2026-10-07

## What

Month view with each day coloured by how well it went.

## Rules

- Green: protein and calories in their green ranges (design/direction.md) and the day's activity logged — at least one activity or "No physical activity"
- Yellow: one condition fails · Red: two or more fail
- A day without entries is grey, not red
- A day with neither activities nor "No physical activity" does not count as logged activity
- Tapping a day opens its detail

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Editing a past entry recolours that day
