# Routine templates

- Status: Approved 2026-10-09 · feature: [F18](features/f18-routines.md)

Low-impact home routines: no jumps, no weight on the hands, short knee range and flat feet (toes are not bent under
load). Values map one to one to `Exercise` in `packages/core/src/energy/routine.ts`.
