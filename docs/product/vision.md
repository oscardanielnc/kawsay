# Vision

- Status: Approved 2026-10-07

## What it is

A mobile journal for food, activity, body and mood that:

1. **Adjusts daily targets to the day you actually had.** A day in bed and a day of stairs and errands do not need the same food
2. **Shows what does not agree with you.** It crosses meals, activity, sleep and stress with bloating, stool, energy and mood
3. **Spots bad habits and suggests changes**, in plain language and on request

## Who it is for

First its author. Later: people with a sensitive gut (bloating, gas, irregular transit) and/or who want to lose
weight and waist without a rigid app.

## What it is not

- Not a medical device. It never diagnoses; it points to a doctor when warning signs appear
- Not a social network, a recipe book or a step counter

## Principles

| Principle | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Under 10 seconds per entry | Taps and drawings first; free text as a shortcut |
| AI picks, the table computes | Nutrients come from food composition tables, calories burned from MET values. The AI only chooses and explains |
| Each user is their own baseline | Insights compare your days with your days, never with a population |
| Private by default | Intimate data is hidden, locked and never sent to the AI |
| Offline first | Every entry works without a connection |
| Your data is yours | Full export of the history at any time |

## Releases

- **v0.1 — start logging:** everything needed to record a full day and see targets and the calendar
- **v0.2 — AI and nutrients:** dishes built by AI, activities in free text, weekly vitamins, minerals and fibre, weekly summary
- **Later:** free-text shortcut (F16), on-demand insights (F17), photo scanner, reminders, Health Connect steps, more languages, public release on Google Play
- **To propose when v1 is defined:** experiments (e.g. "two weeks without dairy" compared before vs during) and a "best and worst days" food view
